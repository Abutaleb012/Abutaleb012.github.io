PhD research · George Mason University · 2015 to 2023

Finding oil spills hidden in hyperspectral imagery

Thin oil on water can look almost identical to the water around it, and linear detectors drown in the noise. My dissertation combined nonlinear kernel-based detectors with Minimum Noise Fraction to pull the slick out. I built four kernel detectors and tested each one against a labeled oil-spill ground truth.

0.76F1 score with kernel OSP and MNF, up from 0.35
84%of oil pixels found, up from 28%
4kernel detectors, all open on GitHub
Code:KSMFKASDKMSDKOSP

Along the way I worked on two USGS hyperspectral mapping projects in Colorado and Alaska, and helped teach a graduate machine learning course on image processing.

Hyperspectral research · 2026

When does desert dust break a target detector?

I implanted synthetic mineral targets into NASA EMIT imagery, degraded the scenes through 6S radiative transfer, then ran five classic detectors against them. Halfway through I found the EMIT water flag was missing part of the Red Sea, so I wrote my own spectral water test and reran everything.

>2.0where MF, ACE, and CEM still hold

Humanitarian GEOINT · 2026

Where should demining teams look first?

Yemen covers 528,000 square kilometers and no team can survey all of it. I combined Sentinel-1 radar, Sentinel-2, SRTM terrain, 47,975 ACLED combat records, and 139,105 road segments into a susceptibility model, and refined it over nine versions.

Marine environment · 2025 to now

Enterprise GIS · 2025 to now

The GIS platform behind Hajj operations

At the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah, through Euroconsult, I manage GIS data for infrastructure across Makkah, Mina, and Arafat: service points, crowd routing, emergency zones, and readiness layers for more than 2 million pilgrims. I audited the ArcGIS Online organization and found 83% of the layers failed the metadata standard, then wrote Python scripts that back up every layer and fixed the metadata automatically.

I also built the PostGIS and ArcSDE geodatabase for infrastructure assets, deployed Field Maps, Survey123, and Dashboards for field teams, and delivered the 1446 versus 1447 season comparison to senior leadership in Arabic.
